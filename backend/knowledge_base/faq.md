# ShopEase Frequently Asked Questions

> Fictional ShopEase demonstration policy. This document is not legal advice
> and does not describe a real business policy.

## What is the return process?

An ordinary return may be requested for an unused and unopened product within
30 calendar days after delivery. The detailed conditions are defined in the
ShopEase Returns Policy. A request is reviewed and is not automatically
approved.

## How long does shipping take?

Orders generally require 1-2 business days for processing, followed by an
estimated 3-7 business days for standard delivery after shipment. The
Shipping Policy contains the detailed estimate. A specific order's status
must come from verified backend order data.

## Can I cancel my order?

A customer may submit a cancellation request while an order has not shipped.
The request is not an approval; ShopEase must verify the order state and make
the decision. Once an order has shipped, the customer should use the return
process after delivery, subject to review of the applicable policy.

## When will my refund arrive?

For an approved refund, ShopEase's fictional processing timeframe is 5-7
business days after approval. A bank or payment provider may take additional
time to post the processed refund. The Refund Policy contains the detailed
distinction.

## What if my product is damaged or defective?

Customers should report damage or suspected defects as soon as reasonably
possible and provide the order number and a description. The seven-day
damaged-on-arrival period is a reporting guideline, not an automatic
eligibility deadline. The Warranty and Product Issues Policy defines the
review process.

## What about payment disputes?

Payment status must be verified from the customer's order data. Payment
disputes, suspicious transactions, and requests to reverse a payment require
human review. This FAQ does not promise a refund or other payment outcome.

## Does ShopEase provide compensation for delays?

The current fictional ShopEase policy corpus does not define discretionary
credits or compensation entitlements. A shipping delay does not automatically
establish eligibility for a credit, percentage, amount, or exception.
Compensation requests require human review.

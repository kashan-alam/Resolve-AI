# ShopEase Returns Policy

> Fictional ShopEase demonstration policy. This document is not legal advice
> and does not describe a real business policy.

## Purpose

This policy describes ordinary returns for eligible ShopEase products that a
customer no longer wants or ordered by mistake.

## Standard return window

An unused and unopened product may be requested for return within 30 calendar
days after delivery. The product should include its normal packaging,
accessories, and included materials.

Submitting a return request does not automatically approve the return or
refund. ShopEase reviews the order and the returned product before making a
final decision.

## Exceptions and separate issue types

Products showing misuse, unauthorized modification, or damage caused after
delivery may not qualify for an ordinary change-of-mind return.

Products that arrived damaged or have a suspected manufacturing defect should
be reported through the damaged or defective product process in the ShopEase
Warranty and Product Issues Policy. Those cases are not automatically treated
as ordinary returns.

## Information required

A customer should provide the order number and a short explanation of the
requested return. ShopEase may request additional information before making a
decision.

## Human review

Human review may be required for exceptions, unclear product condition,
requests outside the standard return window, or any request for compensation
or discretionary credit. The current ShopEase policy corpus does not define
discretionary credits or compensation entitlements.

# ShopEase Shipping Policy

> Fictional ShopEase demonstration policy. This document is not legal advice
> and does not describe a real business policy.

## Purpose

This policy describes the fictional ShopEase order-processing and standard
delivery estimates.

## Processing and delivery estimates

Orders generally require 1-2 business days for processing before shipment.
Standard delivery is estimated at 3-7 business days after the order has
shipped.

These are estimates rather than guaranteed delivery promises. A delivery
estimate does not by itself establish a refund, replacement, credit, or
compensation entitlement.

## Checking an order

An order-status question must use verified order information from the
customer-scoped backend order tool. This policy does not provide the current
location, shipment event, or delivery status of a specific order.

## Delayed or missing packages

A customer may contact ShopEase when an order appears delayed or missing.
ShopEase may request the order number and may require human review to
investigate the delivery issue.

The current fictional ShopEase policy corpus does not define discretionary
credits or compensation entitlements. A shipping delay does not automatically
qualify a customer for compensation, a percentage credit, or any specific
amount. Compensation requests require human review.

## Cancellation and shipped orders

For an order that has not shipped, a customer may submit a cancellation
request. Submitting the request does not automatically approve the
cancellation; ShopEase must verify the order state and make the decision.

After an order has shipped, cancellation is not treated as automatically
approved. The customer should follow the return process after delivery, while
ShopEase reviews any applicable exception.

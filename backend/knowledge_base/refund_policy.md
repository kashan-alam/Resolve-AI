# ShopEase Refund Policy

> Fictional ShopEase demonstration policy. This document is not legal advice
> and does not describe a real business policy.

## Purpose

This policy describes when an approved refund may be processed and how the
processing timeframe should be explained.

## Approved refunds

A refund is considered only after an eligible return or an approved
cancellation. A customer submitting a return or cancellation request does not
automatically receive a refund.

When ShopEase approves a refund, it is sent to the original payment method.
The fictional ShopEase processing timeframe is 5-7 business days after the
refund has been approved.

## Processing time versus posting time

The 5-7 business-day timeframe describes ShopEase's processing time. A bank,
card issuer, or other payment provider may require additional time to post
the processed refund. ShopEase cannot treat a provider's posting time as
ShopEase processing time.

## Refund delays and disputes

A customer who does not see an approved refund after the applicable processing
and provider-posting periods may require human review. Payment disputes,
suspicious transactions, and requests to reverse a payment are not automatic
refund approvals under this policy.

## Compensation and exceptions

The current fictional ShopEase policy corpus does not define discretionary
credits, compensation amounts, percentages, or exception entitlements.
Shipping delays do not automatically establish compensation eligibility.
Requests for compensation or policy exceptions require human review.

# ShopEase Warranty and Product Issues Policy

> Fictional ShopEase demonstration policy. This document is not legal advice
> and does not describe a real business policy.

## Purpose

This policy describes the fictional ShopEase review process for manufacturing
defects and products reported as damaged.

## Manufacturing defects

A manufacturing defect reported within 90 calendar days after delivery may
qualify for warranty review. Qualification is not automatic. ShopEase
reviews the order, the reported problem, and any requested supporting
information before making a final decision.

Normal wear, accidental damage, misuse, and unauthorized modifications are
not covered by the fictional warranty.

## Damaged on arrival

Customers should report products that appear damaged when delivered as soon
as reasonably possible. A seven-calendar-day reporting period is a guideline
that helps ShopEase investigate delivery damage promptly; it is not an
automatic rejection deadline.

Reporting after seven days does not by itself eliminate eligibility. ShopEase
may still review the circumstances, available evidence, and order details.
Final eligibility remains a deterministic backend or human-review decision
where applicable.

## Information required

Customers should provide the order number and a description of the defect or
damage. ShopEase may request photographs or other information needed for
review.

## Outcomes and exceptions

This policy does not promise an automatic replacement, refund, repair,
credit, or compensation. The appropriate outcome depends on verified order
information, deterministic eligibility rules, and human review when required.

The current fictional ShopEase policy corpus does not define discretionary
credits or compensation entitlements. Requests for compensation or policy
exceptions require human review.
